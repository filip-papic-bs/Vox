CONCLUSION
Pharaoh Slot – Consolidated Player Feedback & Recommended Changes
Highest Priority Issues (mentioned by 2+ players)
1. Win animations trigger too often / on very small wins
Mentioned by: Player 2, Player 3, partially Player 4
Problem
Players feel that:
    • animations for wins under 1x are unnecessary
    • they slow down gameplay
    • they reduce excitement because everything feels like a “big event”
    • current system lacks clear distinction between normal win vs big win
Strong Recommendation
Create 3 win tiers with different pacing and intensity:
Win Type	Suggested Trigger	Animation
Regular Win	< 2x	Minimal / instant
Big Win	10x+	Enhanced animation
Mega Win	25x+ or 50x+	Full cinematic
Suggested Changes
    • Remove celebratory animations for wins under 1x
    • For 1x–2x:
        ◦ quick highlight only
        ◦ no interruption
    • Add stronger differentiation:
        ◦ sound
        ◦ screen shake
        ◦ lighting
        ◦ pacing
    • Keep autoplay speed high during small wins
Why This Matters
This directly affects:
    • retention
    • autoplay flow
    • perceived game speed
    • emotional scaling of rewards
Right now players feel the game “overreacts” to small payouts.

⸻

2. Anticipation / teaser spins feel incorrect or confusing
Mentioned by: Player 1, Player 2, Player 3
This is the biggest UX issue in the feedback.

⸻

Core Problem
Players feel anticipation is:
    • triggered in situations where no meaningful outcome is possible
    • happening on unusual reels
    • breaking expected slot behavior
    • feeling bugged instead of exciting

⸻

Specific Issues Identified
A) Reel 5 anticipation after dead Reel 4
Mentioned by: Player 1
Example:
    • Reels 1–3 match
    • Reel 4 misses
    • Reel 5 still delays/spins dramatically
Player expectation:
“Nothing meaningful can happen anymore.”
So the anticipation feels fake/manipulative.

⸻

B) Reel 2 and 3 teaser animations feel wrong
Mentioned by: Player 2
Most slot players expect anticipation:
    • on Reel 4 when Scatter #3 is possible
    • on Reel 5 when 5OAK is possible
Early-reel anticipation feels unnatural.

⸻

C) Reel order / stopping logic feels broken
Mentioned by: Player 1 and Player 3
Example:
    • Reel 5 stops
    • Reel 4 continues spinning
This breaks standard slot rhythm.
Players interpreted it as:
    • a bug
    • synchronization issue
    • “fake anticipation”

⸻

Recommended Anticipation Rules
Recommended Standardization
Scatter Anticipation
ONLY trigger when:
    • 2 scatters are already visible
    • Reel 4 can deliver the 3rd scatter
Optional:
    • Reel 5 only if 4-scatter or 5-scatter rewards exist

⸻

Line Win Anticipation
ONLY trigger on Reel 5 when:
    • 4 matching symbols already exist
    • 5OAK is possible

⸻

Never Trigger Anticipation When:
    • payout outcome is already mathematically capped
    • no upgrade to win is possible
    • Reel 4 already killed the combination
This was the biggest “feels fake” criticism.

⸻

Reel Stop Order Recommendation
Keep strict:
1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5
Avoid:
    • skipped reel timing
    • delayed middle reels
    • Reel 5 stopping before Reel 4
Unless intentionally building a very stylized slot identity.

⸻

3. Bonus round UX needs improvement
Mentioned by: Player 2, partially Player 3

⸻

Problem
Autoplay immediately launches bonus.
Players want:
    • a moment of anticipation
    • a sense of importance
    • control before entering feature
Right now the transition feels abrupt.

⸻

Recommended Solution
Add Bonus Intro Modal
Example flow:
    1 Bonus triggered
    2 Autoplay pauses
    3 Dedicated transition screen appears
    4 Show:
        ◦ Free Spins awarded
        ◦ Feature explanation
        ◦ Multiplier mechanic
    5 CTA button:
        ◦ “START BONUS”
        ◦ “PLAY FEATURE”
This creates:
    • excitement
    • pacing
    • emotional buildup

⸻

Audio Feedback Problem
Current bonus sound:
    • too generic
    • not memorable
    • lacks “reward energy”
Recommendation
Add:
    • rising Egyptian orchestral hit
    • stronger bass impact
    • coin/chime layering
    • vocal element (“FREE SPINS!”)
This is especially important for streamers/content creators.

⸻

4. Free Spins system may be too complicated
Mentioned by: Player 3

⸻

Problem
Players struggle remembering:
    • multiple color combinations
    • different spin values
    • 9 possible outcomes
The mechanic may be:
    • technically interesting
    • but cognitively overloaded

⸻

Risk
If players must:
    • check rules often
    • mentally decode outcomes
Then feature excitement drops.

⸻

Suggested Simplifications
Option A — Reduce combinations
Reduce from:
    • 9 combinations
to:
    • 3–4 meaningful categories
Example:
    • Bronze → 8 spins
    • Gold → 12 spins
    • Pharaoh → 20 spins

⸻

Option B — Additive System
Instead of combinations:
    • each symbol adds spins
Example:
    • Blue = +2
    • Red = +4
    • Gold = +6
Total shown visually.
This is easier to understand instantly.

⸻

Option C — Stronger UI Communication
If keeping current system:
    • display awarded spins immediately
    • visually sum them
    • explain via animation
    • use large numbers/colors
